Sector Applications

Fragmented systems. Workforce transition. Compliance pressure. Oversight complexity increases as automation expands.

Water. Utilities must meet uniform regulatory standards across systems of varying size and capacity. Workforce retirements, fragmented records, and uneven execution increase compliance risk.

Utilities capture evidence at the point of work. TORBU verifies it against defined checkpoints and produces an auditable record for regulators and oversight bodies.

View Water Use Case →

Energy. Distributed crews and vendors execute critical work while regulatory and safety requirements evolve faster than field practice.

TORBU is built to align policy to field workflows, verify critical steps, and preserve supervisory visibility across assets and contractors.

Transit. Safety depends on repeatable inspections, training currency, and auditable handoffs across depots and contractors.

TORBU is built to coordinate roles and validation checkpoints, producing verified records for regulators and post-incident review.

Public Safety. Units must remain synchronized on policy and readiness while decisions stay traceable through command.

TORBU is built to propagate standards down and roll readiness evidence up with clear accountability.

Military. Standardized training and validation must scale across units without breaking hierarchical oversight.

TORBU is built to coordinate execution at scale and provide commanders verified visibility into readiness and compliance.

Why now

Infrastructure statutes formalize what was once discretionary. Regulators tighten certification requirements, mandate asset management, and raise the cost of non-compliance.

AI accelerates execution across systems with no shared accountability. Work fragments. Authority does not follow.

The window to build governed infrastructure is now. The next failure will make the case.
